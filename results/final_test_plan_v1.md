# Frozen final-test plan for the AST revision v5 (drafted 2026-10-16, NOT yet executed)

Status: draft for author approval. Once approved, this file is frozen (hash recorded in the
runner's manifest) and the final-test splits are opened exactly once. Nothing below may be changed
after the splits are opened; any later analysis is labelled exploratory.

## 1. What is frozen

- Encoders: `tge-v2` checkpoints, seeds 42/123/456/789/1024 (main study); mixed-v3 convergence
  checkpoints, seeds 42/123/456 (mixed-domain study). No retraining.
- Calibration: temperature and class prior exactly as in `scripts/workflow_iso_latency_frontier.py`
  (emission-calibration split; training-set prior).
- Detector grids: as stored in `results/workflow/iso_latency_frontier_v1` and
  `results/workflow/bn_glr_frontier_v1` (BN-GLR: D in {24,36}, drift in {0,1}, 14 b0 x 10 b1).
- Feasibility: blind threshold split (steady/switch, x3), exact one-sided 95% upper bound <= 0.05
  per radar stratum (per radar x dynamics stratum in the mixed study).
- Metric: v2 (first correct post-onset alarm; censored delay in seconds).

## 2. Operating-point choice (uses no test flight)

For each method, seed and budget L, the operating point is the blind-feasible point whose
DEVELOPMENT mean switch delay (censored) is <= L and whose DEVELOPMENT feint false-alarm rate is
lowest (ties: lower development delay). The chosen parameter sets are written to
`final_test_v1/selected_points.json` and hashed BEFORE any test split is generated or loaded.

Budgets: degraded radar 24 s and 26 s; nominal radar 22 s and 24 s (main study);
degraded radar 24 s and 26 s (mixed study).

## 3. Methods

Sticky HMM (steady evidence), Sticky HMM (marginal), CRIF (P_S, D60, rho .25), CRIF (P_Z, D36),
W-SGLR (Lau-Tay), CUSUM (marginal), BN-GLR. Mixed study: Sticky HMM, CRIF, CUSUM, BN-GLR.

## 4. Hypotheses and tests (declared in advance)

Primary (main study, degraded radar, both budgets):
- H1: BN-GLR has a lower feint false-alarm rate than the sticky HMM (marginal).
  Test: per-seed paired difference on the same test flights; pooled exact McNemar test over seeds
  (stratified by seed); flight-bootstrap 95% interval of the seed-mean difference.
Secondary (reported with the same statistics, Holm correction across the secondary family):
- H2: BN-GLR vs CRIF; H3: BN-GLR vs W-SGLR; H4: BN-GLR vs CUSUM (expected: no material difference;
  reported as an equivalence-style interval, not as a superiority claim);
- H5: sticky HMM (steady evidence) vs CRIF (same-information ablation);
- feint-to-switch detection, switch detection, false isolation, steady false alarms and mean delay
  for every comparison (no selection on these).
Mixed study (descriptive): subgroup and whole-swarm feint false alarms, feint-to-switch detection.
No claim of BN-GLR superiority on whole-swarm feints will be made unless H1-type tests are
significant there; the expected outcome is that they are not.

## 5. Additional frozen measurements

- Encoder: intent accuracy of the marginal and excursion-vs-steady AUC on test flights, per radar.
- Timing: encoder and BN-GLR wall time per scan on one CPU thread, end to end from tracks to alarm,
  on 200 test flights; hardware and thread count recorded.
- K-class ablation (encoder without excursion classes, trained without feints): trained on the
  existing training split with the same recipe and selected on encoder validation BEFORE the test
  split is opened; evaluated with sticky HMM and CUSUM at the same budgets.

## 6. Reporting

Every number in the manuscript's results sections is regenerated by `scripts/paper_v5_assets.py`
from the final-test outputs; the \devval markers are switched off only after this replacement.
Unfavourable outcomes (e.g. H1 not significant, BN-GLR worse than CUSUM) are reported as found.
